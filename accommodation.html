Where to stay

From beachfront resorts to simple family guesthouses

Mirissa's accommodation is concentrated along the beach road and the quieter lanes just behind it.

Overview

Types of accommodation

Options range from simple family-run guesthouses a short walk from the sand to larger resorts with pools and sea views. Prices below are indicative starting rates for illustration and vary by season.

A few well-known places to stay

Listed for reference only — always confirm current rates and availability directly with the property.

Beachfront resort

Paradise Beach Club Hotel

A pool-and-palm resort set directly on Mirissa beach, popular for sea-view rooms and easy beach access.

Mid-range hotel

Mandara Resort

A comfortable mid-range option close to the main beach, a good base for whale-watching tours leaving early from the harbour.

Guesthouse

Deepika Mirissa Resort

A family-run guesthouse a few minutes from the beach, between Mirissa Beach and Turtle Beach, known for its breakfast views.

Before you book

Tips for choosing where to stay

  • Stay close to the beach road

    Most restaurants, tour operators and the harbour are within walking distance of the main strip.

  • Book early for peak season

    December through February is the busiest and best-value rooms sell out weeks ahead.

  • Ask about noise

    Rooms directly above beach bars can be lively at night — ask for a quieter room if you prefer to sleep early.

  • Check the whale-watching pickup

    Some hotels can arrange harbour transfers for early-morning tours — useful if you don't want to walk in the dark.
